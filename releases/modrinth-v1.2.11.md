## Added

- `Item Quality` (Dungeon)
  - Adds `Quality Bonus: +...% (...)` in dungeon item tooltips
  - Shown directly below rarity line

- `Bazaar/AH Quick Search` (Misc)
  - Hover item + press keybind (default `M`) to quick search
  - Auto-selects `/bz` or `/ahs`

## Changes

- Improved quick-search name cleanup:
  - strips reforges (`Ancient`, `Gentle`, etc.)
  - strips decorative dye/custom symbols
  - sends cleaner item names for search

- Keybind recording UX:
  - `ESC` now sets keybind to `Not Bound`
  - `ESC` no longer closes the config menu while recording
  - `Backspace` also sets `Not Bound`
