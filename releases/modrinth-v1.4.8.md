## Added

- **Warps Shortcuts** (MISC)
  - Warp with `/desert` instead of `/warp desert`, autocompletion included. A shortcut can point elsewhere: `/atoll` warps to `lotus`, `/ch` to `crystals`.
  - The list comes from `api.xenus.fr`, with a bundled fallback so the shortcuts keep working if the API is unreachable.

- **Farming Tools Keys** (MISC)
  - Separate movement and break keys while you hold a farming tool in the Garden, so you no longer have to edit your controls. A toggle keybind turns it on and off in game.

- **Mute Bark Rune** (DUNGEONS)
  - Mutes the wolf growl and bark sounds of the Bark Rune, with an "Only in Dungeons" option.

- **Cosmetics: Custom Wings** (DEV)
  - Dragon wings on players who have them, with a per-player size and color.

## Changed

- **Loadout Title**
  - Highlights the equipped loadout in `/loadout`, as a background behind the item, with a color picker.

- **Pet Level**
  - Color picker for the level text.

- **Item Price**
  - Lowest BIN is no longer shown when hovering an item in the `/loadout` menu.
