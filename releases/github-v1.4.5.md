# XenAddons v1.4.5

Minecraft `26.1.2` • Fabric • Java `25+`

## Added

- **Visitor Garden**
  - Right click a Garden visitor offer to open the Bazaar on the item it asks for. The required amount is copied to your clipboard so you only have to paste it.
  - A yellow reminder is added at the bottom of the offer tooltip while items are still missing.
  - Once you have everything and the offer turns into `Click to give!`, the reminder disappears and the click goes back to its normal behaviour.
