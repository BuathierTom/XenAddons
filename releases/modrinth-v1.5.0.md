## Added

- **Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3 support**
  - Minecraft version (26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3), with every feature on all three.

- **Compact Stash** (MISC)
  - Replaces the stash reminders with one clickable `Item/Material Stash >` line that runs `/pickupstash`.

- **Invincibility Display** (DUNGEONS)
  - HUD with the Spirit Mask, Bonzo's Mask and Phoenix Pet heads showing their invincibility time, cooldown or ready state, a marker on what will save you, and your skins on the heads.

## Changed

- **HUD system**
  - Rewritten: one editor for every element (drag, scroll to scale, snap grid, reset), and the same size whatever your GUI scale.

- **Players Leaped**
  - Reworked, with more zones, a count based on your party size and an "Everyone Leaped!" title with sound.

- **Reminder Gate**
  - The gate is now read from the world, so no more false reminders.

- **Loadout Title**
  - New "Hide None Parts" option, on by default.

- **Invincibility Timer**
  - Renamed from "Invisibility Timer".

- **Default modules**
  - Cosmetics, Config GUI, ZguegAddons and Command Aliases are on by default, and Config GUI can no longer be turned off.

## Fixed

- **Item Price**
  - Lowest BIN for runes and unique runes.

## Notes

- HUD positions are reset: place them again with `/xa hud`.
