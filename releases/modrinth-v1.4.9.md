## Added

- **New Category: FISHING**

  - **Fishing Hook Display** (FISHING)
    - HUD for the Hypixel fishing timer and pull alert, with an editable alert text and the vanilla nametag hidden automatically.

  - **Hotspot Highlight** (FISHING)
    - Highlights fishing hotspots with a color per buff type, outline and/or filled circle, and hides their spawn particles.

  - **Hide Other Bobbers** (FISHING)
    - Hides every other player's fishing bobber.

- **Highlight Pressure Plate** (DUNGEONS)
  - Highlights the I4 Light Weighted Pressure Plate in green while pressed.
