## Added

- **Pet Level**
  - Draws a pet's level in the top left corner of its slot, and hides it once the pet is maxed. The cap is per pet: 200 for Golden and Jade Dragons, 100 for everything else.

## Changed

- **Item Price**
  - Shard prices now show up in the shard menu, not only on shards held as an item.
  - SHIFT multiplies by what you actually hold: the `Stored:` amount in a sack, the `Owned:` amount in the shard menu, instead of a vanilla stack.

## Fixed

- Lowest BIN prices API.
